Privacy policy

rclone-homelab is used only by its owner, to sync the owner's own Google Drive files with the owner's own server using rclone.

Data accessed through Google APIs is stored only on the owner's own devices. It is never shared with, transferred to or sold to anyone, and is not used for any purpose other than that sync.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
